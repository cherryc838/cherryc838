# 🍒 CHERRYC838

**CHERRYC838** is a modern multi-category e-commerce platform designed to offer customers a simple, secure, and convenient online shopping experience.

> **Project Status:** Planning / Pre-MVP  
> **Version:** 0.0.1

---

## 🛍️ About CHERRYC838

CHERRYC838 is being built as an online store where customers can discover and purchase products from multiple categories through one unified platform.

Our goal is to create a modern e-commerce experience focused on simplicity, product discovery, convenience, security, and customer satisfaction.

---

## 🎯 Mission

To make online shopping simple and accessible by bringing useful products from different categories together in one convenient marketplace.

---

## 🔭 Vision

To develop CHERRYC838 into a recognizable e-commerce brand with a growing product catalog, reliable customer experience, and scalable technology platform.
